About Me

Three Things I Can't Sit Still Without

Honestly? I didn't plan any of this. I just started noticing that whenever a teacher handed out a table of numbers, I'd get weirdly excited to poke around and see what was hiding inside it. And whenever I saw a poster that looked off, I couldn't stop thinking about how I'd fix it.

Then there's basketball — the part of my day where I stop overthinking everything and just move. It taught me more about patience than any lecture ever did. Missing ten shots in a row and still going for the eleventh? That's basically the whole skill of getting good at anything.

  • Data I like the moment a boring spreadsheet suddenly starts telling a story.
  • Design Spacing, color, and fonts — I'll spend an hour on 2 pixels and be happy about it.
  • Basketball The place where my head finally goes quiet. Number 30, usually the loudest on the court.
